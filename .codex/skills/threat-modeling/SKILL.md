# Threat modeling

Define scope/assets/actors/data classes; diagram flows and trust boundaries; enumerate STRIDE plus business/tenant/LLM abuse; rank likelihood and impact; select preventive/detective/recovery controls and tests; assign owner/status and residual risk. Include supply chain and human approval. Update risk register. Complete when each material threat maps to a control/test or explicit human risk acceptance.
