State date, currency, region and price sources. Model low/base/high traffic with fixed and variable drivers: requests/CPU/memory, minimum instances, DB/storage/backups, egress, logs/traces, model tokens/tools and human operations. Include free tiers cautiously, taxes/FX uncertainty, unit cost and runaway scenario. Recommend budgets/alerts/quotas/max instances/caching or alternatives. Never authorize spend; complete with assumptions and owner.
