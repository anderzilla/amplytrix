# Architecture decision record

Orient first. Describe context and decision drivers; compare viable alternatives including doing nothing; record decision/status/date, positive and negative consequences, reversibility, security/cost/operations, validation and trigger to revisit. Use the next number in `docs/adr`, update its index and affected diagrams/docs. Never rewrite an accepted decision; supersede it. Complete after consistency and link checks.
