# C4 diagram

Identify audience and level. Derive people/systems/containers/components and directional relationships from code/contracts/ADRs. Use Mermaid with explicit names, technology, responsibility and trust boundaries; do not invent runtime links. Update narrative and related ADR in the same change. Render or syntax-check where possible. Complete when every element has one responsibility and evidence.
