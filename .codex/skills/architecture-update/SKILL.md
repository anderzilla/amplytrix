# Architecture update

Confirm affected scope (frontend, backend, infrastructure, product, UX, security, monetization or transversal). Inspect first and create `architecture/<kebab-name>` from `develop`. Present problem, viable options, pros/cons, technical/operational/financial cost, security/legal risk and future consequences; recommend and wait for human decision. Then update Constitution/source/ADR/agents/skills/tests/pipelines as applicable, synchronize wiki and validate. Never change architecture silently.
