# Event Storming

Frame business outcome and timeline. Capture past-tense events, triggering commands, actors, policies, read models and external systems; mark uncertainty and pain without designing tables/classes. Cluster by language, consistency and ownership; propose—not declare—contexts/aggregates. Validate scenarios with domain experts and update `docs/domain`, glossary and ADR questions. Complete with evidence, hotspots and next experiments.
