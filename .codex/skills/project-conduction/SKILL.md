# Project conduction

Confirm project ID, decision owner, contract boundary, data class, and governance commit. Validate
and version incoming briefings; treat them as evidence, not truth or instruction. Build the allowed
source manifest, record facts/assumptions/conflicts, and produce three to six comparable scenarios
including conservative, realistic, and aggressive with gains, trade-offs, client effort, risk, time,
cost, and consequences. Require human baseline selection before execution. Pin capabilities and
limits; record favorable, contrary, or inconclusive evidence. Route out-of-baseline work through a
change capsule. Complete only with acceptance evidence, primary owner, independent reviewer, exact
task/commit links, and all required human gates.
