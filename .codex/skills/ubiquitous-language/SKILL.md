# Ubiquitous language

Collect real terms/examples and identify synonyms, overloaded words and context-specific meanings. Define term, invariant/examples, owning context and prohibited conflations. Rename docs/contracts/code together when accepted; never force one global meaning across legitimate contexts. Complete when scenarios read consistently and unresolved conflicts have owners.
