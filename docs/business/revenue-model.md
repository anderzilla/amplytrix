# Revenue model

Revenue candidates: recurring plans, configured add-ons, prepaid credits, premium onboarding and professional services. Costs include payment fees/tax uncertainty, Cloud Run/SQL/storage/observability, AI provider usage and human operations. Measure MRR/ARR/ARPU/conversion/churn/expansion together with gross margin by plan/capability and trust guardrails.

Unit economics use `gross_margin = recognized_revenue - payment_fees - allocated_cloud_and_AI_cost - variable_support`. Currency, tax, FX and recognition rules require finance/legal confirmation; credits are liabilities/accounting questions until reviewed.
