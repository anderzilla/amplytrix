# Pricing hypotheses

No final price is approved. Seed data may carry explicitly non-final hypotheses by market, currency, billing period and effective window; production publication requires product, finance, legal and commercial-integrity approval. Existing subscriptions retain their referenced price version unless a disclosed, lawful migration is accepted.

Test value-based differentiation across individual productivity, team collaboration and architecture governance; compare monthly with transparent annual savings. Founder offers are versioned, eligibility-bound and auditable, with clear duration and migration. Stop on material trust, refund, chargeback, complaint or margin guardrail breach.
