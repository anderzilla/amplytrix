# Business model

Subscription hypothesis with optional team/enterprise contracts and curated catalog value. Validate willingness to pay, retention, support cost, AI gross margin, Brazilian taxes, chargebacks, and enterprise procurement before pricing. Meter model/tool usage independently from provider billing so margin and portability remain visible.

# Governance addition

Commercial hypotheses must identify value, recurring revenue or cost contribution, primary and trust guardrail metrics, unit economics, legal/security/reputation risk, test and abandonment criteria. Retention comes from recurring value, never artificial dependency. Current normative rules are in `docs/source-of-truth/monetization.md`.
