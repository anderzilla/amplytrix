# Commercialization

Supported surfaces are pricing/comparison, monthly/annual checkout, trials, truthful coupons/promotions/founder offers, add-ons, credit packages, contextual upgrade, downgrade/cancel/resume, billing portal, payment recovery and renewal/trial notices. Professional services and referrals remain optional/future.

Every experiment records segment, value hypothesis, evidence, conversion metric and guardrails. Price, currency, period, tax/fee treatment, renewal, limit, overage, cancellation and promotion conditions appear before consent. No fabricated urgency, silent conversion, obstructed exit or unsubstantiated “unlimited” claim is permitted.
