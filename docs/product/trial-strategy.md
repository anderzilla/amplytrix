# Trial strategy

Default hypothesis: seven days, once per organization and verified identity, no automatic charge without explicit consent, visible start/end, pre-expiry notices, simple conversion and a retention window rather than immediate data loss. Eligibility and policy are versioned configuration.

Experiments may test no-card, explicitly consented card, 7/14 days, invite-only and assisted premium variants through flags. Guardrails include complaints, refund/chargeback, cancellation, abuse false positives and trust. Abuse controls minimize data and provide appeal; invasive fingerprinting requires privacy/legal review.
