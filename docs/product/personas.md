# Personas and jobs

| Persona                      | Job to be done                                           | Success signal                                     |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Developer                    | Understand, implement, test, and document changes safely | Lower cycle time without defect growth             |
| Tech lead                    | Standardize reviews and unblock a team                   | Consistent quality and predictable flow            |
| Architect                    | Preserve decisions and system coherence                  | Traceable trade-offs and fewer boundary violations |
| Engineering manager          | Govern delivery, risk, and capability                    | Visible risk/value and reusable practices          |
| Security/platform specialist | Embed guardrails and operability                         | Earlier findings and lower response time           |
