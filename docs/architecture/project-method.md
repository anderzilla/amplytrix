# Project method and boundaries

## Intake and readiness

Inputs may originate from an Agency briefing, client documents, third-party documents, or direct
discovery. Agency is the preferred prepared path, not a mandatory purchase. All material is
untrusted evidence until the internal CodeMatrix readiness check establishes minimum completeness,
coherence, risks, and gaps. Agency-originated handoff additionally records source/version, transfer
basis or consent where applicable, evidence, and idempotency.

The minimum check estimates effort, cost attribution, and commercial disposition for human approval.
It is not the full paid Agency diagnostic. Material gaps route to a proposed paid Agency diagnostic
or complementary Amplytrix discovery phase. The system never creates price, discount, contract,
charge, payable, or payment.

## Decision and execution

Discovery produces three to six comparable scenarios. The client's recorded selection creates an
immutable baseline. Project Governance admits pinned capabilities and work; Execution Orchestration
can act only inside those constraints. Outside work creates a change capsule and pauses until human
decision. Evidence & Supervision requires favorable/contrary evidence and independent review before
acceptance.

## System ownership

CodeMatrix Agency owns commercial education, its diagnostic product, and corporate management.
Amplytrix owns project delivery records and runtime. A future Agency handoff uses a versioned API or
event derived from `schemas/v1/project-intake.schema.json`, with separate identities/stores and no
shared database. The Command Center reformulation is a future consumer project of this method and is
implemented only in its owning repository.
