# ADR-0046: Stripe international adapter

Status: proposed — 2026-08-01

## Context and decision

Prepare a Stripe adapter for approved international markets, inactive until demand and entity/legal review. A single global provider reduces code but increases lock-in; simultaneous launch adds needless scope.

## Consequences and validation

Preserves expansion at adapter/test cost. Validate canonical mappings and parity only before enabling a market.
