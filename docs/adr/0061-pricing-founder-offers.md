# ADR-0061: Configured pricing hypotheses and founder offers

Status: accepted — 2026-08-01

## Context and decision

Initial prices are labeled hypotheses in versioned seed/configuration, never code or final claims. Founder offers have explicit eligibility, benefit, duration/migration and audit. Secret/perpetual ambiguous discounts were rejected.

## Consequences and validation

Enables learning while protecting trust. Finance/legal/commercial-integrity approval gates publication; monitor conversion with refund, chargeback, complaint, cancellation and margin guardrails.
