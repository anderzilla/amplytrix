# Privacy

Apply LGPD-oriented purpose limitation, minimization, transparency, lawful basis, consent where applicable, retention, access/export/deletion and processor governance. Personalization and experiments disclose relevant purposes and do not silently reuse data. Refusal must not trigger unjustified degradation, and consent cannot be disguised, improperly preselected or bundled unnecessarily. Abuse controls avoid invasive fingerprinting and opaque sensitive automated decisions without legal review; legitimate users receive recovery/appeal. No production personal data or credentials enter local tools, repositories, prompts or logs. Legal review remains required before collection, vulnerable-group targeting or material automated decisions.

Project knowledge is segregated and provenance-linked. While a project is active, identifiable data is
retained only for documented purpose and policy. Closing/exporting a project does not authorize reuse:
CodeMatrix may retain only patterns proven effectively anonymized and legally reusable, with evidence
of the process. Until legal and technical validation exists, reuse and derived-pattern retention are
blocked. Credentials, banking access, and out-of-scope data never enter project retrieval.
