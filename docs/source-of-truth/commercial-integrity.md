# Commercial integrity

Review pricing, checkout, trials, renewal, cancellation, upgrade/downgrade, offers/coupons, billing messages, onboarding, gamification, notifications, social proof, comparisons, landing pages, campaigns, experiments and personalized recommendations for truth, material disclosure, consent, autonomy, reversibility, privacy and capability accuracy.

BLOCKER/CRITICAL/HIGH findings block homologation: deception; illegal, abusive or immoral practice; dark pattern; false urgency/scarcity/social proof/metric/message; obscure charge/renewal; inadequate consent; harmful targeting/gamification; artificial dependency; privacy/autonomy violation; deliberately difficult cancellation/export/downgrade; or claims beyond product capability. A review records element, technique, intended outcome, manipulation/legal/reputation risk, revenue/retention impact, recommendation and verdict. See ADR-0041.
