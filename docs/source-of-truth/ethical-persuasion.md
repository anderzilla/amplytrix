# Ethical persuasion

Ethical persuasion helps a user understand and choose using true benefits, verifiable evidence, honest comparison, reduced uncertainty, clear consequences and preserved refusal/cancellation. Allowed techniques include authorized social proof, documented demonstrations/cases/benchmarks, transparent price anchoring and savings, real guarantees/trials, relevant recommendations, legitimate negotiation, and factual urgency or scarcity.

Every recommendation records technique, user benefit, business benefit, required evidence, manipulation risk, ethical boundary, success metric, guardrail metric, less-aggressive alternative and final recommendation. Strong emotion and direct language are allowed; fear without basis, guilt, shame, confusion, disguised consent, artificial pressure and exploitation of insecurity or vulnerability are not. Human review is mandatory when conscious choice may be impaired. See ADR-0041.
