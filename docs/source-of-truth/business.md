# Business

Features must measurably support acquisition, activation, conversion, retention, revenue, expansion, referral, churn reduction, cost reduction, or differentiation. Track MRR, ARR, ARPU, LTV, CAC, payback, conversion, activation, retention, churn, expansion, upsell, cross-sell, useful usage, referral and revenue by capability alongside complaints, refunds, chargebacks, immediate cancellations, failed/reversed charges, checkout confusion, support, reports, accessibility, consent, deletion/export requests, satisfaction, trust and transparency perception. Hypotheses require an owner, segment, duration, evidence, legal/privacy impact, guardrails and stop condition. Never optimize revenue alone. See ADR-0040 and ADR-0041.

Every Amplytrix project passes a minimum internal readiness check. Validation of client or
third-party documentation is estimated and attributed as Agency process within the proposed
Amplytrix composition; it is not invisible or automatically free. A full Agency diagnostic remains
a separately paid deliverable. Material gaps route to a proposed Agency engagement or complementary
Amplytrix discovery phase. Systems may record effort, calculate scenarios, and propose a commercial
disposition, but only authorized humans approve price, discount, contract, charge, obligation, or
payment. Phase Zero defines no fixed hours, price, or automatic accounting transfer.
