# Monetization

Be commercially aggressive through demonstrated value, faster activation, transparent plan differentiation, contextual premium discovery, honest trials, relevant upsell/cross-sell, real incentives, useful history/integrations/automation/personalization, and measurable outcomes. Acceptable revenue maps to outcome, capacity, convenience, savings, security, speed, scale, governance, integration, support or strategic value—not artificial frustration.

Prices show amount, currency, billing period, taxes/fees where applicable, limits, renewal, promotion conditions, overage behavior, cancellation and add-ons before consent. Anchors, discounts, deadlines and scarcity must be real and fairly comparable. Trials disclose conversion and charge timing. Price/plan changes require advance transparent notice and consent where required.

Every proposal states user/business benefit, persuasion technique, hypothesis, commercial and guardrail metrics, risk, cost, legal/privacy/reputation impact, experiment, evidence, success and stop criteria. Refusal, downgrade, cancellation and export remain clear and reasonably symmetric with signup. Dark patterns, involuntary conversion, artificial exit costs and frustration-based charging are prohibited. See ADR-0040 and ADR-0041.
