# Monetization

Be commercially assertive through demonstrated value, faster activation, transparent plan differentiation, contextual premium discovery, honest trials, useful history/integrations/automation/personalization, and measurable outcomes. Every proposal states user/business benefit, hypothesis, metric, guardrails, risk, cost, legal impact, experiment, success and abandonment criteria. Consent, price, renewal, usage limits, downgrade, cancellation and export remain clear. Dark patterns and artificial exit costs are prohibited. See ADR-0040.
