# Product

Amplytrix amplifies engineering professionals through governed agents, skills, automation, templates, and technical intelligence. It assists human judgment and competes through superior real outcomes, delivery speed, experience, differentiation, personalization with consent, transparent pricing and trust. Every feature states persona, problem, benefit, commercial objective, hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, expected impact, cost, risk, monetization/retention strategy, evidence, success and abandonment criteria. Revenue cannot depend on confusion, involuntary payment, impaired exit, false information or user vulnerability. See ADR-0040, ADR-0041 and `docs/templates/feature-specification.md`.
