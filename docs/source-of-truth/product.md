# Product

Amplytrix is a project intelligence and delivery platform. It turns incomplete or dispersed
information into a traceable source of truth, comparable scenarios, a human-selected baseline, an
executable plan, and governed delivery. It coordinates agents, skills, tools, retrieval, evidence,
and approvals without replacing accountable human judgment.

The first MVP is a CodeMatrix-managed end-to-end service and does not depend on a complete customer
portal. A reviewed-package modality follows; subscription self-service is later. Every engagement is
organized by project and its documented contract, rules, limits, sources, roles, and acceptance
criteria. Existing code and artifacts are primary evidence for an existing project; a guided
interview and supplied references initiate a new project.

Agency briefing is the preferred prepared intake, not a mandatory prior purchase. Client documents,
third-party documents, and direct discovery are also accepted. CodeMatrix performs a minimum
internal readiness check for completeness, coherence, risk, and gaps before Amplytrix execution.
That gate is not the same as providing the Agency's complete commercial diagnostic/documentation;
the complete diagnostic remains a paid, independently deliverable product. When client/third-party
material is checked, the internal validation effort is estimated and attributed to the Amplytrix
commercial composition as Agency process rather than hidden/free work. Material gaps require a paid
Agency engagement or an approved complementary Amplytrix discovery phase. No agent sets a price,
discount, charge, payable, or payment.

Amplytrix produces three to six comparable scenarios, including conservative, realistic, and
aggressive. The client selects; the recorded decision forms the baseline for scope, price, schedule,
and execution. Work outside that boundary becomes a change capsule and cannot silently expand agent
authority.

Every feature and project states user value, commercial objective, evidence, cost, risk, guardrails,
success, and stop criteria. Revenue cannot depend on confusion, involuntary payment, impaired exit,
false information, or vulnerability. See ADR-0040, ADR-0041, ADR-0062, and
`docs/templates/feature-specification.md`.
