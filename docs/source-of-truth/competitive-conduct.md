# Competitive conduct

Amplytrix competes aggressively through product quality, real outcomes, speed, clearer communication, documented comparisons, legitimate guarantees, education, service, experience, price/value justification, trust and innovation. Competitor dishonesty, piracy, false advertising or abusive conduct never authorizes imitation.

Competitive comparisons identify date, scope, source, methodology, limitations and owner; avoid cherry-picking and remain reproducible. Never use unauthorized logos, fictitious companies/users, edited demonstrations that hide failure, or unverified claims. Turn competitor misconduct into a trust advantage through superior evidence. See ADR-0041.
