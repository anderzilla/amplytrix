# User communication

Communication may be strong, confident, direct, emotional and memorable when it remains technically accurate and audience-appropriate. Claims about time, errors, productivity, process, automation, exclusivity, competitive advantage or value require contextual evidence and material limitations. AI is never presented as infallible or a guaranteed replacement for professionals.

Titles, controls, errors, notifications, billing, onboarding and upsell state cause, impact, action, price and recovery without blame or unnecessary internals. Never fabricate statistics, users, activity, claims, urgency, scarcity, testimonials, reviews or alerts; omit material limits/cost/renewal; guarantee untested outcomes, absolute security or error-free operation; use incomplete comparisons; exploit professional insecurity; or use guilt, fear and confirmshaming. Social proof must be real, authorized, verifiable, contextualized and representative.
